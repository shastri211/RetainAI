# RetainAI

### Predict. Explain. Retain.

## Overview

RetainAI is an AI-powered customer churn prediction platform designed to help businesses identify customers who are likely to leave their services before they churn.

Unlike traditional churn prediction projects that only classify customers as likely to churn or not, RetainAI provides explainable AI insights, business-oriented analytics, and actionable retention recommendations to support data-driven decision-making.

The platform combines machine learning, explainability, interactive visualizations, and a web-based interface to transform predictive models into a practical decision-support system.

## Problem Statement

Customer acquisition is significantly more expensive than customer retention. Businesses often identify customer churn only after customers have already left, resulting in revenue loss and missed retention opportunities.

Organizations need an intelligent system capable of identifying at-risk customers early, understanding the reasons behind their churn probability, and recommending effective retention strategies before customers decide to leave.

## Solution

RetainAI combines machine learning with explainable AI to predict customer churn while providing meaningful explanations for every prediction.

The platform analyzes customer information, estimates churn probability, identifies the key factors influencing the prediction, and recommends practical business actions that can improve customer retention.

By transforming predictive analytics into actionable insights, RetainAI helps businesses make informed decisions instead of relying solely on prediction scores.
